Feature 01

The Community Intelligence Engine

A new member connects GitHub instead of filling out a form. OrbitOS reads their languages, contribution history, and skill level, and builds a living profile — no manual setup.

From there, it flips the usual flow. Instead of members searching for opportunities, opportunities find the members: the right mentor, a matching open-source issue, a relevant event — the moment they join.

Feature 02

Autonomous Team Formation

At most hackathons, the moment registration closes, people are still posting "anyone need a frontend developer?" in chat.

With OrbitOS, that coordination is already done. The instant registration closes, it analyzes every participant's skills, timezone, and availability, and assembles balanced teams automatically — no manual matchmaking required.
